    About Crypto Toolkit

    Crypto Toolkit is a comprehensive suite of cryptocurrency utilities designed for traders, developers, and blockchain enthusiasts. Our mission is to provide fast, accurate, and beautifully designed tools that help you navigate the crypto space with confidence.

    All calculations are performed locally in your browser. No data is sent to any server, ensuring your privacy and security. The application works offline for most tools, making it reliable even without an internet connection.

    Key Features

    • 20+ specialized crypto tools
    • 100% client-side processing
    • Offline support for local tools
    • Live market data via CoinGecko API
    • Multi-language support (English & Arabic)
    • Light and dark themes

    Privacy Policy

    Last Updated: July 2026

    1. Information We Collect

    Crypto Toolkit does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All calculations and operations are performed locally in your browser. No wallet addresses, passwords, or financial data is sent to our servers.

    2. Cookies & Local Storage

    We use browser local storage to save your preferences (theme, language, favorites). This data remains on your device and is never transmitted externally.

    3. Third-Party APIs

    Live price data is fetched from CoinGecko public API. No identifying information is included in these requests.

    4. Security

    While we strive to ensure accuracy, you should always verify critical calculations independently. Never enter real seed phrases or private keys into any online tool.

    For privacy-related questions, contact us via Telegram.
